= Send a notification
:description: Send a notification and check the delivery record.

include::glossary:partial$attributes.adoc[]

In this tutorial, you will send a {gt-notification} with your {gt-template} and channel preference, and look up its delivery {gt-record}.

== Prerequisites

include::ROOT:partial$try-out-key-prereq.adoc[]
* You have the {gt-template} `id` from the xref:tutorials:first-notification/create-template.adoc[Create a template] tutorial
* You have a {gt-recipient} identifier and a channel preference from the xref:tutorials:first-notification/set-channel-preference.adoc[Set a channel preference] tutorial

== Send the notification

With a template and an allowed channel preference in place, you can now send a notification to the {gt-recipient}.

=== Step 1: Send the notification

. Open the _Send notification_ endpoint below.
. Fill in the request following the example request payload or schema.
+
include::ROOT:partial$rapi-doc-mini.adoc[tag=send]
+
. Click *Try* and *Execute* to send the notification.
. See the endpoint response and copy the `recordId` for the next step.

=== Step 2: Look up the delivery record

. Open the _Retrieve record_ endpoint below.
. Fill in the path with the `recordId` from the previous step.
+
include::ROOT:partial$rapi-doc-mini.adoc[tag=records-get]
+
. Click *Try* and *Execute* to look up the delivery record.
. See the endpoint response with the delivery record `status` set to `delivered`.

You have successfully completed the tutorial and sent your first notification!
